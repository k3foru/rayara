The question

Every automation estate now faces the same choice

Bots are cheap to run but break on messy input. Agents handle judgment but add cost, risk and audit questions. Most teams are asked to “add AI” without a rule for which work goes where.

01

Brittle bots

Exceptions pile up wherever input is unstructured: emails, free-text notes, scanned or foreign-language documents.

02

Ungoverned pilots

Agent demos that call a paid model on every record, with no cost ceiling, no data controls and no audit trail.

03

No decision rule

Nobody can say, process by process, whether to keep the bot, add ML inside it, or rebuild it as an agent.

The Rayara Tech approach

One decision per process: keep, augment or go agentic

Keep as RPA

Stable, rule-based work

Structured input, fixed logic, few exceptions. Harden it, schedule it well and leave it alone.

Augment with ML

Bots that need judgment at one step

Embeddings, rerankers or a trained model at the decision point, with confidence scores and human review flags.

Go agentic

Multi-step reasoning across systems

Planner and executor agents with memory, escalating to a paid model only when confidence fails, and logging every step.

Every recommendation comes with a measured run cost and an accuracy baseline, not a demo.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask first

How much does a project cost?

Fixed quotes for defined scope. An assessment is a short fixed-fee engagement; pilots and builds are quoted after the assessment so the number reflects real systems and volumes. Send a brief and you get a range within 24 hours.

How long does a typical project take?

Assessments take about two weeks. A single-process pilot usually runs 4–8 weeks; larger builds 8–12 weeks, delivered in working increments.

Who owns the code and the bots?

You do. Client data and code stay with the client, and accelerators are built fresh for each engagement.

Do we have to replace our existing RPA platform?

No. Most of the value comes from keeping bots that work and adding ML or agent steps only where judgment is needed.

How do you handle security and sensitive data?

Data stays inside your environment where required, PII is tokenized or masked before it reaches any external model, and agent actions are logged to an audit trail.

Can you work with our team and time zone?

Yes. Based in Bangalore, India with overlap for UK, EU, Gulf and US East Coast teams, and comfortable working inside your tools and meetings.

What happens after go-live?

You get documentation, tests and a handover. Many clients add a monthly run-and-improve retainer covering monitoring, retraining and the next process in the queue.
